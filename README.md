## 🔬 Current Research Work

I’m actively exploring performance improvements in **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)** for fully categorical datasets. My research areas include:

- **Similarity Metrics:**  
  Focus on Hamming Distance, Simple Matching Coefficient (SMC), Jaccard, and related non-Euclidean measures.

- **Goal:**  
  Evaluate how manual **attribute selection (removal of 9–11 columns)** influences performance, with the objective of making **Hamming distance outperform SMC and Dice** under controlled conditions.

- **Process:**  
  - Structured experiments in Python  
  - Clean Jupyter notebooks  
  - Comparative accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 validation  
  - Reproducible setups for repeat experiments  

I emphasize **explainability, transparency, and reproducibility**, keeping every experiment well-organized and easy to understand.
